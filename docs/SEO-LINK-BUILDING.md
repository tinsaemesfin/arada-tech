# Link building and backlinks (off-site SEO)

Backlinks are links from other websites to **aradatech.com**. They help search engines assess trust and relevance. This checklist complements on-site SEO work in the codebase.

## Quick wins

1. **Complete social profiles** — Add your official Facebook, X, Instagram, YouTube, and LinkedIn URLs in [`src/data/company.ts`](../src/data/company.ts). Each profile’s “website” field should point to your site.
2. **Google Business Profile** — Claim and verify if you have a physical service area; link to the site.
3. **Partner and client links** — Ask satisfied clients, integrators, and vendors for a short “Built by Arada Tech” or partner link on their site.
4. **Directories** — Relevant Ethiopian / African business and tech listings (quality over spam).
5. **Guest posts and PR** — One useful article on a reputable industry or local business site often beats many low-quality links.

## What to avoid

- Paid link farms, comment spam, and irrelevant directories.
- Exact-match anchor text in bulk (keep anchors natural: company name, URL, or “software company in Addis Ababa”).

## Measure

- Use Google Search Console “Links” (or your SEO tool) to watch **referring domains** grow over months, not days.
